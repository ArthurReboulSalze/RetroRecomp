# libchdr (the CHD decoder of the PC Engine CD)

A PC Engine CD game holds its disc as a CHD and reads it through [libchdr](https://github.com/rtissera/libchdr) at the revision pinned in
`retrorecomp/pcecd_build.py` (`LIBCHDR_REV`), linked statically into the game: the game ships no DLL. The library is fetched at that revision when a
conversion needs it (no copy in this repository) and built with its own bundled decoders, whose notices follow. The texts below are unmodified
from that revision (the notice of miniz is the header of its source file; the zstd text is the `LICENSE` file of its release 1.5.7, of which the
BSD licence is the one taken: the library offers BSD or GPL-2.0, at the user's option).
