# X.Org Misc Fixed 10x20 and 6x10 (game menu fonts)

Game menus embed the printable ASCII subsets of X.Org's Misc Fixed 10x20 and
6x10 bitmaps, both released into the public domain. Original notice:
"Public domain font.  Share and enjoy."

The original notices, sources and checksums are retained in
`native/font10x20.h` and `native/font6x10.h`.

Sources:
https://sources.debian.org/src/xfonts-base/1%3A1.0.5%2Bnmu1/font-misc-misc/10x20.bdf/
and
https://sources.debian.org/src/xfonts-base/1%3A1.0.5%2Bnmu1/font-misc-misc/6x10.bdf/
