# Credits music and playback

The three four-channel MOD files in MEDIAS/trackers_chiploom were supplied by
Arthur Reboul Salze for this credits screen and created with ChipLoom:
https://github.com/ArthurReboulSalze/ChipLoom

They are played directly from their original patterns and samples, not from
WAV/MP3 recordings. ChipLoom is credited as the creation tool; its code license
does not relicense the audio contained in independently supplied modules.

The converter-only player statically includes libxmp-lite 4.7.3 and the device
output part of miniaudio 0.11.23. Source URLs and SHA-256 pins are recorded in
tools/build_credits_audio.py. It is not linked into generated games.

## libxmp-lite
https://github.com/libxmp/libxmp/tree/libxmp-4.7.3
